Trading Style Note

These four styles are defined in the backtest engine and sent to the AI backtest. The rule layer uses score, RSI range, stop loss, and take profit thresholds; DeepSeek receives the selected style with daily market candidates and returns simulated BUY/SELL/HOLD orders. Current AI mode still relies on the model to interpret the style name, while backend limits enforce max positions, allocation, confidence, and valid market symbols.

MomentumTrend-following baseline. Score >= 4, RSI 42-72, stop -7%, take +18%.
GrowthMore tolerant of volatility for larger upside. Score >= 3, RSI 38-78, stop -10%, take +24%.
ConservativeStricter risk control and earlier exits. Score >= 4, RSI 40-65, stop -5%, take +12%.
High volatilityHigher-risk mode with wider movement bands. Score >= 3, RSI 35-82, stop -12%, take +30%.
